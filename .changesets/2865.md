- fix(deploy): Only delete release directories when cleaning up old baremetal
  deploys by @Tobbe

The cleanup step of `cedar deploy baremetal` only removes release directories
directly under your app's `path`, identified by their 14-digit timestamp names
(`YYYYMMDDHHmmss`). The newest `keepReleases` releases are kept. Anything else
in `path`, like the `current` symlink, `.env`, or a persistent `uploads`
directory, is left untouched.

Releases deployed with a custom `--releaseDir` name that isn't a 14-digit
timestamp are not removed by the cleanup step, so you need to delete those
yourself.
