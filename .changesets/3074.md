- fix(api): a sandboxed child can require() past a node_modules on the way by
  @Tobbe

A sandboxed child (`@cedarjs/api/sandbox`) no longer fails with
`ERR_ACCESS_DENIED` when a CommonJS lookup in it (`require()`,
`require.resolve()` — pdf.js loading its canvas, a CJS package loading its
dependencies) passes a `node_modules` folder that exists but doesn't hold the
package, like the `api/node_modules` Vite and Vitest create for their caches.
Those folders are now readable to the child; the one holding the package stays
closed apart from the packages themselves.
