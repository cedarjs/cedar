- fix(jobs): Cap retry backoff so failing jobs can't crash the worker by @Tobbe

The delay before a failed job is retried (`attempts ** 4` seconds) is capped at
7 days, so jobs with a very high attempt count always get a valid retry time
instead of an invalid date that crashes the worker.

If the jobs adapter throws while recording a job's error or failure, the error
is logged and the worker keeps running. A job that has reached `maxAttempts` is
still marked as failed when recording its final error throws. After such a
write error the worker waits `sleepDelay` seconds before looking for its next
job, so a job that is still locked by the worker isn't re-run in a tight loop.
