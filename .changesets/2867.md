- fix(jobs): Cap retry backoff so failing jobs can't crash the worker by @Tobbe

The delay before a failed job is retried (`attempts ** 4` seconds) is capped at
7 days, so jobs with a very high attempt count always get a valid retry time
instead of an invalid date that crashes the worker.

A job that reaches `maxAttempts` is marked as failed right away, with its final
error recorded, without first scheduling a retry. If the jobs adapter throws
while recording a job's error or failure, the error is logged and the worker
keeps running.
