- feat(tenancy)!: permissions as the only org check (#3221) by @Tobbe

Step 1 of #3219: in an app with tenancy, permissions are the one check on the
current organization.

## What changes

- **`@requireOrgAuth` and `requireOrgAuth()` are gone.** `createTenancyAuth()`
  returns `hasUserRole` and `requireUserAuth`, and `setup tenancy`
  no longer writes the directive. Fields about the current organization use
  `@requirePermission`. "Any member" is a permission every role lists,
  `organization.read`, so a role added later starts with nothing until its entry
  in the map says otherwise.
- **`requirePermission` takes a string:** `requirePermission('members.manage')`
  instead of `requirePermission({ permission: 'members.manage' })`. The
  directive is unchanged.
- **`ROLE_GRANTS` is replaced by a subset rule.** `createPermissions()` also
  returns `canGrantRole(role)`: the signed-in user may give someone `role`, or
  take it away, when they hold every permission it has. The generated
  `inviteMember` and `removeMember` use it, so nobody can hand out more than
  they may do themselves, and a new role needs no grant table. The field's own
  `@requirePermission(permission: "members.manage")` still decides who may
  manage members at all.
- **The default map gets an owner-only permission,** `organization.manage`.
  Without it, owner and admin would be the same bundle and an admin could make
  someone an owner.

  ```ts
  permissions: ['organization.read', 'organization.manage', 'members.manage'],
  roles: {
    owner: ['organization.read', 'organization.manage', 'members.manage'],
    admin: ['organization.read', 'members.manage'],
    member: ['organization.read'],
  },
  ```

- **Generators:** in an app with tenancy, `generate sdl`/`scaffold` (and the
  realtime and uploads setups) write
  `@requirePermission(permission: "organization.read")` for a model that belongs
  to an organization. Model-specific permission names are step 3 of #3219. A
  tenancy app is now recognised by its `requireUserAuth` directive.
- **`<PrivateSet orgRoles>` and `hasOrgRole` are gone** too, on the api, on the web and in `useCurrentOrg()`, so no check in an organization goes by role. Use instead:
  - for a route, `<PrivateSet orgPermission>`;
  - in the current organization, `hasPermission()` / `useAuth().hasPermission()`;
  - in another organization, that membership's `permissions`;
  - to check membership, `getCurrentOrg()` / `useCurrentOrg()`.

  Showing a role still reads `membership.role`.

## Breaking (canary-only tenancy APIs)

- An app that already ran `setup tenancy` on a canary has to:
  - drop `requireOrgAuth` and `hasOrgRole` from its `createTenancyAuth()` destructuring;
  - delete `api/src/directives/requireOrgAuth`;
  - move its `@requireOrgAuth` fields to `@requirePermission`.
- `requirePermission({ permission: 'x' })` becomes `requirePermission('x')`, in
  services and in `api/src/directives/requirePermission`.
- `ROLE_GRANTS` in `api/src/services/organizations` becomes `canGrantRole(role)`
  from `src/lib/permissions`.
