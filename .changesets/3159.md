- feat(tenancy)!: roles from the current organization, and fail closed (#3159)
  by @Tobbe

In an app with tenancy, `@requireAuth` is replaced by two directives that say
what a field is about. `@requireOrgAuth` needs a current organization, and
checks the user's role in it. `@requireUserAuth` needs a signed-in user only,
and checks `currentUser.roles`, as `@requireAuth` did. `yarn cedar setup
tenancy` renames the app's existing `@requireAuth` fields to
`@requireUserAuth`, since there were no organizations before, and
`cedar generate sdl` and `scaffold` pick `@requireOrgAuth` for models with an
`organizationId`. `useAuth().hasRole` inside an `OrgScope` checks the role in
that organization too, and `OrgScope` takes `roles` and `unauthorized` to guard
routes by it.

`createTenancyAuth()` from `@cedarjs/tenancy` gives an app's
`api/src/lib/auth.ts` its `hasRole`, `hasOrgRole`, `requireOrgAuth` and
`requireUserAuth`. A bare `@requireOrgAuth` admits any member of the current
organization, as a bare `@requireAuth` admits any signed-in user.

`@requireMembership` and `requireMembership()` are removed. To upgrade an app
set up with them, see "Upgrading from `@requireMembership`" in the tenancy
docs.
