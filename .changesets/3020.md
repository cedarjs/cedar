- feat(uploads): derived files (variants) for thumbnails, previews and poster frames (experimental) (#3020) by @Tobbe

`@cedarjs/uploads` can keep track of files made from an upload: thumbnails, a
PDF's drawn first page, a video's poster frame. Declare them with
`defineUploadVariants()`, one generator function per variant name, and Cedar
stores each as an `Upload` row linked to its source. A missing variant is made
on first read, once even when several requests or processes ask at the same
moment; a variant that can't be made is remembered and retried after
`retryFailedAfter`; `regenerateVariants(name)` remakes every variant of one
name; and `deleteFile()` with `targets` deletes a source's variants with it.

The package makes no images itself and depends on no media libraries. The
feature needs two columns, a self-relation and a unique index on the `Upload`
model; see "Derived files" in the uploads docs. It is experimental and may
change.
