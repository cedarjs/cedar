- fix(forms): coerce extended date-only years at UTC midnight (#3283) by @razzeee

`DateField` converts the input's `YYYY-MM-DD` value to a `Date` at UTC midnight for every year the input accepts, including years with five or more digits. Invalid dates, such as `2026-02-30`, become `Invalid Date`. `Date` values that come from `setValue()` or `defaultValues` are kept as they are.

A `DateField` with `validation={{ valueAsDate: true }}` honors `emptyAs`. An empty field submits `null` by default.
