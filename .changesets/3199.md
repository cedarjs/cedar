- feat(structure): record tenancy guards on routes (#3199) by @Tobbe

The structure package's view of a route now includes `unauthorized`,
`userRoles`, `orgRoles` and `orgPermission`, read from the enclosing `<Private>`
/ `<PrivateSet>` elements next to `roles` and `unauthenticated`. They are also
in the route list of `getProjectRoutes` and in the route manifest. Nothing
enforces anything from them.
